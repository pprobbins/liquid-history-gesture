Liquid History Gesture

Try this on a phone, tablet or browser with touch emulation.

  1. Tap Add history entry.
  2. Swipe right from the left edge to go back.
  3. Swipe left from the right edge to go forward.
  4. At the newest entry, swipe from the right edge again to see the grey wave.

Short swipes retract. Vertical scrolling and ordinary taps remain available.
